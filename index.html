Meet Your Surgeon

Dr. Sadia Saif

Consultant Dental Surgeon | Orthodontist & Implantologist

Dr. Sadia Saif graduated from the prestigious Karachi Medical and Dental College (KMDC) in 2006. With over 16 years of dedicated clinical practice, she has established herself as one of DHA Karachi’s premier dental professionals.

Her clinical expertise encompasses advanced Orthodontics, Dental Implantology, Cosmetic Dentistry, Periodontics, and both Hard & Soft Tissue Management. She is renowned for her delicate, pain-free approach and commitment to custom treatment planning.
